Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Overview

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the DocuBox app for iPhone and iPad. DocuBox has no accounts and no servers of its own: the developer never receives your files, your passwords, or anything about how you use the app.

What We Collect

Nothing. DocuBox does not collect, store, or share personal information, and it contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code. Specifically, there is:

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No account, sign-in, or registration
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone

Your Files

Your files are stored on your device. They leave it only when you send them somewhere yourself: to a server you added, to a nearby device you approved, to another app through the share sheet, or to a printer. Folders you keep in DocuBox also appear in the Files app, on the same device. If you have iCloud Backup or device backups turned on, Apple includes app data in them under your Apple Account; that is controlled in iOS Settings, not by DocuBox.

Servers You Add (SMB, SFTP, WebDAV)

When you add a server, DocuBox connects directly from your device to that server and nowhere else. Passwords and keys are kept in the iOS Keychain on your device. What the server records about your connection is up to whoever runs it. SFTP and WebDAV over HTTPS are encrypted in transit; SMB is encrypted when the server supports it and you turn encryption on, and WebDAV over plain HTTP is not encrypted.

iCloud Sync

DocuBox uses Apple's iCloud to keep your favorites, app settings, and saved server details (such as names, addresses, and usernames) the same across your own devices. Server passwords are not synced. This data is stored in your private iCloud account by Apple; the developer cannot see it. It applies only if you are signed in to iCloud.

Nearby Sharing

Nearby sharing sends files directly between devices running DocuBox on the same local network. Devices are paired with a PIN, each transfer has to be accepted, and transfers are encrypted. While you use it, your device's name is visible to other DocuBox users nearby. Nothing passes through a server.

Built-in Web Browser

The browser loads the websites you visit directly from your device. Those sites, and the search engine you choose in Settings (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, or one you set), receive your requests and searches as they would in any browser and handle them under their own privacy policies. Bookmarks, open tabs, cookies, and other website data stay on your device, and you can clear website data in the app. The developer does not see what you browse or download.

Device Permissions

DocuBox asks for a permission only when a feature needs it, and each can be changed in iOS Settings:

  • Camera: to scan documents into PDFs. Scans and text recognition are processed on your device.
  • Photos: to import items you pick with the system photo picker, and to save a photo or video to your library when you ask. DocuBox does not browse your library.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: to unlock folders you have locked. Authentication is done by iOS; DocuBox never receives biometric data.
  • Local network: to find nearby devices and reach servers on your network.
  • Notifications: to tell you about transfers and incoming sharing requests.

Search, Siri, and Widgets

File names and text are indexed on your device so you can find them in the app and in Spotlight; locked folders are left out. Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, and Control Center controls work with the same on-device data. None of it is sent to the developer.

Children

DocuBox does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because it includes an unrestricted web browser, the app is rated 17+.

This Website and Contact

If you write to us through the contact page, the name, email address, and message you enter are delivered by a third-party form service (FormSubmit) and used only to reply to you. This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

DocuBox has no accounts, no tracking, and no servers of its own. Your files go only where you send them, and the developer never sees them.
